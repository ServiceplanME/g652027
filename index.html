01 Where we are

The events are cancelled. The launch still needs a room, a moment and an audience.

What changed

Customer and media events are off for safety reasons. The launch moves to social, in a premium keynote format.

The format

A scheduled premiere on IG, YouTube and X with a countdown and a live chat, then boosted through media.

Where and when

Sulaymaniyah showroom, Riyadh. Premiere on Sunday 8 November 2026.

SetSet mockup: one highlight screen, an interview area to the left, the car to the right.
02 The approach

Shown, never claimed.

Leading with confidence means the car does the talking. The show has to feel live, keep people watching until the reveal, and leave a library of content behind it. Three principles hold it together.

It plays live

A countdown before every stream, a host who talks to the audience, and a chat that is looked after in real time. Pre-recorded, never feels it.

The screen is the second host

The highlight screen behind them has its own story: abstract before the reveal, a landscape that feels like home after it.

Built to be cut

Every segment is shot to stand alone, and the same day delivers two car reels and a full set of stills.

05.1 The countdown

Nobody joins a black screen.

The livestream will begin in

10:00

Ten minutes early

Every platform goes live 10 minutes before the film, straight into this countdown with a low ambient score.

Same on every platform

English and Arabic, 16:9 for YouTube and X, 9:16 for Instagram. The booking link is pinned before the film starts.

Announced before

An IG story countdown sticker from three days out, so followers get a reminder the moment it starts. YouTube's Premiere page counts down too.

05.2 How the signal travels

One master, three platforms, one control room.

YouTube runs as a true Premiere: scheduled, played to everyone at once, and the home of the evergreen video afterwards.

Instagram goes out as a Live, fed with its own vertical version of the film, subtitles placed for the phone.

X goes out as a scheduled broadcast from the same feed. Live chat on all three.

05.2 How we go live

Software, kit and steps.

The film is pre-recorded, so the stream can run from a control room anywhere with a strong connection, including Dubai.

What we need

PlayoutvMix on a main and a mirrored backup machine. Plays the countdown, then the film, and sends a separate stream to each platform.
YouTubeYouTube Studio. The film is uploaded and scheduled as a Premiere, with the 10 minute countdown at its head.
InstagramInstagram Live Producer on desktop, which gives the stream key for the 9:16 output.
XX Media Studio Producer, a scheduled broadcast with its own stream key. Access confirmed on the account a week before.
ConnectionWired line with at least 50 Mbps upload, plus a bonded 5G backup.
MonitoringOne screen per platform watching the public stream, and the chat team in each platform's own tools.

Steps

  1. Lock the masters: 16:9 and 9:16, subtitles burned in, sound mixed for streaming.
  2. Set up the three streams and load the keys into vMix. Pin the booking link in each description.
  3. Rehearse end to end two days before, on private or test accounts.
  4. One hour before: control room on, backup machine mirrored, speed test.
  5. Ten minutes before: go live on the countdown. The YouTube Premiere counts down on its own.
  6. On air: the film plays. The chat team posts, pins and answers.
  7. After the film: hold the end card, then end the streams. If the Q&A runs, hold for 15 minutes.
  8. Afterwards: replays stay up on all three, the YouTube countdown is trimmed, the link stays pinned.
